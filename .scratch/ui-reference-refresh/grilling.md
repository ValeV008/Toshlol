## Confirmed decisions

1. **Visual authority:** the supplied images in `UI_pics_new/` replace the older visual direction. Match their layout, spacing, typography, and colors closely: red Expense headers, green Income headers, blue Budget headers, off-white backgrounds, flatter lists, and colored editor rows. The user corrected the initial folder name to `UI_pics_new` and later supplied three additional Split references. Q12/Q14 explicitly retain the three bottom actions from `UI_pics_old/overview.jpg` as an exception.
2. **Finance rules:** preserve existing finance behavior, including optional Categories, persistent Account selection, monthly calculations, independent saved Budget scopes, and Transfer accounting. Screenshot appearance does not override these rules. New functionality pictured in the references requires separate decisions.
3. **Whole-app consistency:** apply the new visual language to screens without new references, including Overview, Settings, Account management, and setup. Their accepted layouts and neutral-screen palette are recorded below.

4. **Navigation and primary actions (Q4, refined by Q12/Q14/Q15):** use the left drawer to switch between Overview, Expenses, Incomes, Budgets, Categories and Tags, and Accounts. Remove the Overview/Expenses/Income navigation tabs. Retain the three visible bottom actions Add expense, Add income, and Transfer money shown in `UI_pics_old/overview.jpg` on Overview, Expenses, and Incomes. The user calls this a bottom drawer; the supplied image shows a fixed bottom action bar, not an expandable panel. These actions replace the directional screens' reference plus buttons. Budgets keeps its own plus. Do not add the proposed Overview plus chooser or a replacement Accounts-screen Transfer action.
5. **Transaction details (Q5):** tapping a Transaction opens details before editing, with a prominent amount, metadata, optional Note, edit/delete controls, and existing deletion confirmation.
6. **Extra features (Q7):** include Split functionality, with its meaning and lifecycle confirmed below. Omit the other proposed extras: free-text search, conversion of a Transaction to a Transfer, location, recurring Transactions, reminders, photos, and per-Transaction currency changes. Keep ordinary Transfer creation and existing currency configuration.
7. **Transaction editor (Q8):** use the four colored Category, Tags, Account, and Date rows, opening pickers. Tapping the large amount opens a numeric keypad; Aa opens the Note editor. The currency circle displays the configured currency rather than changing currency for a Transaction.
8. **Right drawer (Q9/Q19/Q20/Q25):** retain month selection, individual Account switches, and an All accounts switch. Remove the bottom action row, including Add transfer and Edit accounts. Retain monthly periods, persistent empty selection, and month-end Closing balances; arbitrary date ranges remain excluded. All accounts selects only active Accounts. The Budgets clock opens this same right drawer: changing Account switches leaves the Budget screen and its calculations unchanged but updates the shared selection used on returning to Overview, Expenses, or Incomes. Do not replace it with the proposed month-only picker. Saved Budget scopes remain independent.
9. **Budget presentation (Q10):** use a conventional spent-divided-by-limit progress bar with explicit remaining/overspent text. Omit the reference's unexplained black marker and Non-monthly heading. Existing monthly Budget rules remain in force.
10. **List grouping (Q6/Q18):** include By date and By category views inside Expenses and Incomes. By date is the initial view and groups activity chronologically by day. By category summarizes matching Transactions by Category for the same direction, month, and selected Accounts, ordered largest total first; tapping a Category opens its matching activity. Include an Uncategorized group. Transfers remain visible in By date and are absent from By category and its totals.
11. **Split meaning and appearance (Q11/Q16/Q22):** divide one recorded Expense or Income into two or more parts, each with its own optional Category, Tags, and Note. All parts share Account, date, and Income/Expense direction. Creating the Split must preserve the original amount exactly; for example, a EUR 60 Expense becomes EUR 40 Groceries and EUR 20 Household. Show parts as separate rows, each marked by the reference's gray branching Y-shaped symbol; do not substitute a single collapsed payment row. Details show the opened part's own amount and metadata, then a Part of split expense section (or Income equivalent) with the current group total and related part rows. Related rows are tappable and open that part's details.
12. **Settings access (Q13):** add a Settings gear at the bottom of the left drawer, separate from the six primary destinations. Settings retains currency configuration, Export, Import, and reset with existing confirmations.
13. **Split calculations (Q17):** match Budgets against individual parts. A EUR 60 Split of EUR 40 Groceries and EUR 20 Household contributes EUR 40 to the Groceries Budget, EUR 20 to Household, and EUR 60 to an unrestricted matching Budget. Monthly totals and Account balances count the money once, without also counting an unsplit original or group total. Existing Tag compatibility and independent/overlapping Budget scope rules still apply.
14. **Archived Account visibility (Q21):** treat archived Accounts as soft deleted: hide them from the right drawer and remove them from saved main Account selections. Preserve their read-only history under Accounts -> Archived. Existing Budget scopes and historical spending remain intact, as do finance records and Backup data. This supersedes the older requirement allowing archived Accounts to remain selectable in the main drawer, including when viewing earlier months.
15. **Unsplit (Q22/Q33):** draft a recombination of all related parts into one Transaction for their current combined amount, opening an editor to choose its final Category, Tags, and Note. The final classification is a user choice; do not silently discard distinctions by choosing an arbitrary part's fields. Preserve the shared Account, date, and immutable direction. Commit the entire change only on Save; cancellation leaves the existing parts unchanged.
16. **Per-part edits and deletion (Q23):** amount, Category, Tags, and Note edits affect only the opened part. Deleting a part removes only that part and retains the existing deletion-confirmation behavior. Recalculate the current group total: changing EUR 40 to EUR 45 in a EUR 40 + EUR 20 Split produces EUR 65. If one part remains after deletion, it becomes an ordinary Transaction. The exact-original-total constraint applies when creating a Split, not as an immutable total after later edits.
17. **Shared-field edits (Q24, corrected by Q35):** changing a Split part's Account or date applies to every part in that Split, with this effect clearly stated in the editor. Income/Expense direction cannot change at all; Q35 supersedes the earlier proposal to apply a direction change across the group. Preserve active-Account, positive-amount, direction-specific Category, and compatible-Tag rules across all affected parts.
18. **Overview (Q26):** use a blue header and the new off-white/white visual system. Retain, in order, the selected Accounts' combined Closing balance, monthly Income/Expenses/net, Account cards, Budget progress, and recent activity. Keep the three bottom actions. No fabricated percentage changes or update timestamps are introduced.
19. **Budget list and editor (Q27):** give each Budget its own white card showing name, limit, month, saved scope, progress, and remaining/overspent amount. Plus opens a separate creation screen; tapping a card opens editing, replacing the current always-visible editor below the list. The header reads All budgets / selected month, and its clock opens the shared right drawer. Account switches must not change this header or Budget content.
20. **Pickers and save/cancel (Q29):** Category, Tags, and Account open bottom sheets; Date opens a calendar. Category permits None, Tags permits compatible multiple selections, and Account lists active Accounts. Use one top-right save check and a top-left cancel button. Ask before discarding unsaved changes. Omit the duplicate bottom save check pictured in the ordinary editors.
21. **Secondary destinations (Q30):** use blue headers and simple white lists/forms for Accounts, Categories and Tags, Settings, and setup. Accounts has Active and Archived views; Categories and Tags has separate tabs. Transfer entry uses a large amount and From Account / To Account / Date fields, with no classification fields. Preserve existing functions, finance rules, and confirmations, including read-only archived history and Note-free Transfers.
22. **Long content and larger fonts (Q31):** wrap text, grow layouts vertically, and stack the three bottom actions when needed, while matching the references closely at ordinary font sizes. Keep names, amounts, controls, and validation messages readable without clipping or overlap.
23. **Split creation reference and adaptations (Q28/Q32):** follow expense_split_edit.jpg: Split toolbar with cancel/save, shared Account and date above a Split into section, and two initial vertically stacked white part cards. Each card has an Amount / Set amount row followed by colored Category and Tags rows. A remaining-from-total amount appears below the cards. Add Add part, a remove control on each part, and Aa for each part's optional Note; Amount opens the ordinary numeric keypad. Save requires at least two positive parts summing exactly to the original total; unset draft amounts are permitted while editing, not on Save. Use corresponding green tones for Income. Use Part for the domain concept despite the reference's Entry labels.
24. **Draft boundaries (Q33):** Split creation and Unsplit remain drafts until the final save check. Cancelling either leaves all original Transactions, amounts, classifications, and group relationships unchanged. Use the agreed unsaved-changes prompt when a dirty draft is discarded. Ordinary editing retains its existing save/validation semantics.
25. **No repeated Split (Q34):** ordinary Transaction details offer Split. Existing Split-part details offer Unsplit, with no Split-again action. To replace an allocation, Unsplit and then Split the resulting ordinary Transaction. Do not introduce nested Splits.
26. **Immutable direction (Q35):** the user explicitly rejected Expense/Income conversion. Add expense always creates an Expense; Add income always creates Income. Do not show a direction selector or permit changing direction during ordinary editing, Split, part editing, or Unsplit. Category choices remain appropriate to the fixed direction; changing Category still removes incompatible Category-associated Tags under existing rules.
27. **Empty and error states (Q36):** show simple contextual empty messages, such as No expenses this month. Place validation errors beside their relevant fields and preserve the draft for correction. An empty Account selection retains zero main-ledger totals and no selection prompt. Budget content remains independently scoped.

## Design tree

- Visual authority: settled (new references).
  - Navigation and primary actions: primary drawer destinations, Settings footer, and three bottom actions on Overview/Expenses/Incomes settled. Budgets keeps its plus.
  - Transaction list grouping: By date default, descending Category totals with drill-down, separate marked Split rows, and Transfers only in By date settled. Free-text search excluded.
  - Transaction details and editing flow: details-first, separate Split-part details, tappable related parts, Unsplit, per-part amount/classification/deletion, group-wide Account/date, no nested Splits, and draft commit behavior settled.
  - Editor interaction and optional tools: colored picker rows, amount keypad, per-part Note, Add/remove part, fixed currency/direction, bottom-sheet pickers/calendar, single save check, cancel, and dirty-draft prompt settled.
  - Account/month drawer controls: month selector, individual switches, active-only All accounts, no bottom actions, active-only visibility/selection, and Budget-clock access with shared selection but unaffected Budget view settled.
  - Budget presentation and progress meaning: spent/limit bar, remaining/overspent text, separate per-Budget cards, full-screen list/editor separation, and independent header settled.
  - Designs for destinations without references: Overview layout, blue neutral headers, simple white lists/forms, Active/Archived Accounts, Categories/Tags tabs, and Transfer amount/Account/date layout settled.
  - Empty, error, long-content, and larger-font layouts: contextual empty states, draft-preserving field errors, wrapping/growth/stacked actions settled.
- Finance rules: settled (preserve existing rules, extending classification through Split).
  - Split: shared Account/date, immutable direction, independently classified separate rows, exact-sum creation, per-part Budget matching, editing/deletion, draft-only cancellation, and Unsplit settled. Backup compatibility and atomicity remain inherited constraints; storage/migration design belongs to later implementation planning.
  - Archived Accounts: hidden from the main drawer and removed from saved selections; read-only history and existing Budget scopes/spending preserved. This supersedes the earlier selectable-archive requirement.
  - Other screenshot-only feature additions: excluded as listed above.
- Scope: settled (whole app; documentation only).
  - Q37 clarified workflow rather than approving a rewrite of the implemented baseline; the later explicit to-spec invocation authorized synthesis into a separate specification.
  - Specification creation: completed in the new effort through to-spec; the user subsequently invoked to-tickets and approved publication of 14 separate tickets. Do not rewrite the implemented baseline.

## Constraints and intentional adaptations

- Existing native Android 10, offline, light-only, local data, archive, validation, and Backup constraints remain applicable.
- Existing Categories have text names, with no Category-specific artwork or icon picker. Generic field icons shown in the references do not imply adding Category artwork.
- Transfers remain separate from Transactions and excluded from Income, Expense, net, and Budget spending. Reference sample amounts are not finance acceptance values.
- Budgets remain calendar-month recurring definitions with independent saved Account scopes. The pictured Non-monthly & tag budgets heading does not establish new recurrence behavior.
- The black Budget-bar marker has no established meaning and is excluded. Neither the screenshot bar fill nor its sample overspending numbers defines calculations.
- Split allocation and its accepted lifecycle are in scope as described above. Conversion of Transactions into Transfers, location, recurring Transactions, reminders, photos, per-Transaction currency selection, search, and arbitrary date ranges are excluded.
- Current code opens Transaction editing directly and has no separate details screen; the accepted details-first flow changes this interaction.
- Current code uses Overview/Expenses/Income tabs and three labeled bottom actions. Drawer navigation replaces the navigation tabs, while Q12/Q14/Q15 retain the three bottom actions on Overview, Expenses, and Incomes.
- The baseline spec explicitly allowed archived Accounts in the main drawer and financial selection. Q19/Q21 deliberately supersede that behavior with active-only selection and Archived history access. Preserve historical records, existing Budget scopes/spending, and Backup data.
- Existing implementation verification describes the previous UI. It does not verify the new reference design.

## Accepted clarification: By date and By category (Q6)

This concerns the two tabs pictured within Expenses and Incomes, not navigation between those screens. The user accepted the following example: Monday has Groceries EUR 30 and Transport EUR 10; Tuesday has Groceries EUR 20. By date shows those day groups; By category shows Groceries EUR 50 and Transport EUR 10, with each Category opening its matching activity. Both views retain the same selected month and Accounts. Uncategorized activity remains accessible. Transfers cannot contribute to Category spending/earnings totals under the existing finance rules.
